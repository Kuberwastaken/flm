# FLM / ChatFLM

FLM is the architecture and research project. ChatFLM is its browser workspace.
Spell the expanded name “Fly Language Model.” Use “next-token predictor” or
“research prototype” when describing the current compact checkpoint's capability.

The original fly mark lives in `public/mark.svg`. The interface uses IBM Plex Sans,
warm white (`#faf8f5`), dark neutral text (`#292820`) and burnt orange (`#a74c20`).
Research comparisons use orange for FLM, muted green for GRU and purple for the
transformer. Keep labels alongside colors.

The share visual is generated by `python scripts/brand_visuals.py` after `npm ci`.
Its points are projected anatomical coordinates, with a documented source hash.
Orange identifies the modeled subset, not neural activity. The interactive brain
uses actual inference states and has its own signed activity legend. Neither
visual is evidence of biological cognition or trained motor behavior.

Keep editorial claims tied to measurements. Do not imply that the current model
outperforms transformers or that the anatomical fly has acquired human knowledge.
